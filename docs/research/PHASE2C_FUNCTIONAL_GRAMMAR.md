# Phase2C grammar from full-content reviews

All39 available TXT derivatives were read completely, including corrupted/blank
endings; all16 reconstruction-only targets were reviewed against both complete
annotated mockups and two-page brochures. Specifications now record16 conscious
analytical answers each, hash/representation-group/line/page locators and claims
requiring conversion review. Completion is **analysis of available evidence**,
not recovered audio, listener approval, precise timing or factual efficacy.

## Recurrence and justified components

Most surviving learning shells orient to purpose, settle an uninterrupted supported
posture, temporarily set aside concerns, enter ten, open eleven learning context,
teach target association, close to ten, then offer sleep/consolidation and waking
return/reminder. These support composable purpose/preparation/association/future-use/
learning-closure/return operations, not copied boilerplate or a universal track.
Explicit target mental operations differ materially from broad improvement
propositions with an optional intensifying cue. Instruction repetition is not
automatically listener rehearsal, and ASR repetitions are not accepted underlay.

Important exceptions: Access to Energy includes explicit inner exploration and
post-close imaginative practice; Access to Information includes an explicit
retrieval exploration but lacks an ending. Do This Now uses an intention-oriented
introduction and post-close ten-state rest rather than an established sleep
section. Passages has distinct closing/reopening phrasing, optional gratitude and
a second waking sequence. Sleep pairs its cue with exhalation rather than the
usual hold/exhale routine. Missing endings remain unknown, not inferred returns.

## Families useful for future authoring (not historical identities)

- Target-selective retrieval/encoding/alternatives: Recall, Imprint, Access to
  Information, Options; Contemplation adds explicit OPEN/CLOSED control.
- Persistent enhancement with optional intensification: Think Fast, Buy the
  Numbers, Speaking, Seeing, coordination and several organ-oriented claims.
- Conditional reduction/release: Attention narrows a task; Let Go and Off-Loading
  reduce selected emotional/thought salience; De-Hab conditions use on pattern onset.
- Adjustable two-mode targets: Hearing, Nutricia and Sex Drive are documented
  in available instruction; missing Smell/Taste/Touch rely on brochure modes and
  mockup procedures. Seeing's available source is single intensification, so a
  sensory label does not establish the same two-mode grammar.
- Sleep goal variants: sleep entry, restoration claims, dream target, problem
  incubation, short-nap restoration; Sleep Easy is catalogue-supported but audio
  operations remain reconstructed, not the OCF pilot's inferred rest design.
- Catalogue-only conditional limits: Light Foot requires foot movement; Injury
  has a specifically injury-caused-unconsciousness autoactivation assertion;
  Strong-Quick/Stay Awake are restricted temporary applications. No broader
  automatic/emergency mechanism is generalized.

Release catalogue describes suspension with learning retained, not erasure.
Contemplation directly has CLOSED; Zoning's CANCEL exact role is ambiguous and
RELEASE-after-need is documented separately. These justify explicit distinct
future suspension/close concepts, not installing a universal reset command.
Body, immunity, regeneration, detoxification, diagnostic, foresight and sensory
range claims remain classified assertions requiring human purpose/safety review.
No physiological mechanism becomes established merely by semantic extraction.

Generation/narrator/date differences are not authenticated from these derivatives.
Video/copyright/religious insertions and implausible counts are documented as
contamination, not silently normalized or made into doctrine. The16 pamphlets
provide identity/purpose/use restrictions, not the missing recordings' exact
opener/main/closer. Shared shells in mockups are related-function inference.
The new[generated matrix](../FUNCTION_EXTRACTION_REVIEW.md) distinguishes those
bases; all specifications retain draft/pilot and human-review requirements.

# Phase2B corpus relationships and structural grammar census

All277 source objects were hash-checked against the prior inventory. Full text
representations were parsed in memory; PDFs were inspected with pdftotext and ZIP
members read/hashed without filesystem extraction. No source/reference file was
modified and no source wording is published in this report or runtime records.

`phase2b-corpus-relations.json` records source/content hashes, representation groups,
PDF heading categories, ZIP member identities and exact duplicate matches.
There are39 normalized-content groups (punctuation/layout/case ignored after
format parsing). They establish shared word sequences, not independent historical
evidence, timing identity, semantic equivalence or authentication.

50 PDF objects have command plus purpose/description headings supporting a
secondary pamphlet-content classification. Five PDFs remain insufficiently
classified. Counts after this **document-type refinement**:107 source-designated
transcripts,10 partial derivatives,33 mockups,84 secondary documents,43 unknown.
Authenticated original-recording transcripts remain **zero**. The earlier
inventory/report is preserved as the previous knowledge state.

## Broader structural census

Across the39 operator-designated TXT function derivatives, candidates occur in:
preparation39, command association38, future use37, closer36, opener35, main34,
return32. Candidate absence is not proof of semantic absence: ASR gaps, line
breaks and different formulations affect detector coverage. These numbers are
not confidence percentages or a substitute for complete human functional review.

Preparation/association/future-use/closing are strong recurring **structural
candidates** across the full surviving-text corpus. Exact wording, automatic
installation claims and long ASR repetition are not adopted as OCF infrastructure.
Main operations remain function-specific; reusable stages must not replace those
operations with a generic explanation. Existing purpose/command/future stages
remain useful. Unknown suspension/reset and consolidation behavior stays unknown.

Canonical domain metadata distinguishes sensory, sleep, cognitive, body-oriented,
emergency and other research families. Command mode metadata identifies genuinely
declared adjustable pairs without inferring identity from titles. A release versus
enhancement taxonomy and generation/version differences are not yet established
by a reviewed cross-corpus semantic analysis. No recording generation/date is
deduced from filenames, OCR headers or stylistic similarity.

All55 functional specifications remain **partial**. Evidence now has
hash-addressed related representations and refined pamphlet types; keyword
operation candidates remain structural inference, not accepted function semantics.
The16 source-unavailable targets retain metadata plus explicitly reconstructed
research. Their opener/main/closer operations must not be invented merely to fill
empty blocks. Complete function-specific extraction remains independent follow-up;
it must not use frozen Hearing/Sleep Easy decisions as approved policy.

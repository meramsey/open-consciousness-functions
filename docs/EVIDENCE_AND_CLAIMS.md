# Evidence and Claims

OCF distinguishes *what is described* from *what is proven* — and rarely shows
the latter.

## Evidence states

| State                | Meaning                                              |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| `source-described`   | Documented in legacy/source material                 |
| `community-hypothesis` | A community working hypothesis, not demonstrated   |
| `anecdotal`          | Personal reports, no aggregate evidence              |
| `experimental`       | Formal experiments exist (must cite them)            |
| `externally-supported` | Supported by an external, citable body of work    |
| `unverified`         | Currently unverified by any controlled method        |
| `pending-source-review` | Ownership/resolution of the source not settled    |

## What OCF never claims

OCF functions, suggestions, meditation, binaural beats, Farfield presets,
hypnosis, or audio entrainment do **not** guarantee any psychological,
physiological, medical, paranormal, or behavioral result. Statements to the
contrary are a defect and should be reported (see `SECURITY.md`).

## Intended vs prohibited claims

Each record distinguishes:

- `claims.intended_effects` — what the function *aims* to do, phrased as
  intent ("supports a felt sense of …"), never as guarantees;
- `claims.prohibited_claims` — statements that are never acceptable
  (diagnosis, treatment, cure, paranormal guarantee, etc.).

## Health functions

Health-oriented functions require an explicit safety statement, defaults to:

> Self-directed wellness practice; not a substitute for professional medical
> evaluation or treatment. No guaranteed effects are claimed.

## Emergency functions

Emergency-oriented functions must explicitly state they do **not** replace:

- emergency services;
- first aid;
- poison control;
- medical evaluation;
- professional treatment.

Completion wording from the record's safety notes is echoed in matrix output
and any future audio scripts.

## Anecdote vs. evidence

Testing reports (`ocf new test-report`) and audio transcripts are anecdotal
records. They belong on the *claims/evidence* fields, not in marketing copy.
Label them accordingly.

# Editorial approval boundary

The four registered functions listed in
[Previously approved editorial proposals](EDITORIAL_CANDIDATES.md) retain their
approval decisions and hash-only evidence in canonical per-locale resources.
The approval applies to the presented narration, cues, procedures, and Intuition
registration. It does not authenticate historical source material, establish
rights clearance, approve listening, or authorize release.

Canonical English resources are:

- [`OCF-EXP-001`](../locales/en-US/OCF-EXP-001-Manifest.yaml)
- [`OCF-EXP-003`](../locales/en-US/OCF-EXP-003-Dissolve.yaml)
- [`OCF-EXP-005`](../locales/en-US/OCF-EXP-005-Clear-Zone.yaml)
- [`OCF-EXP-006`](../locales/en-US/OCF-EXP-006-Intuition.yaml)

Prior wording is recoverable from ordinary Git history. Current preview, command
discovery, session planning, and integrity checks read canonical locale data;
they do not load or reconstruct candidate snapshots.

# Welcome to OCF

## What OCF is

This listening guide is a starting point, not a promise of a particular experience.
You remain in charge: pause, skip, or stop whenever you wish. OCF is a declarative
function and audio authoring project; a catalog entry is not proof of an effect,
a finished recording, or release approval. This is editorial guidance, not
reviewed session narration or medical advice.

## Choosing a function

Start with one purpose that matters to you. Read its function guide, including
source availability, implementation status, and precautions, before choosing
a recording. A planned function may not have a usable listening edition.

## Commands and cues

Function guides distinguish exact display cues from exact spoken cues. Spelling,
hyphens, and spoken wording can differ. A cue identifies a command; it does not
by itself supply an activation procedure. Where reviewed instructions are absent,
the guide says so rather than guessing breathing, repetition, or timing steps.

## A comfortable environment

Choose a place where you can safely give the recording your attention. Sit or
lie comfortably with support, and reduce avoidable interruptions. Do not listen
while driving, operating equipment, or doing anything that requires vigilance.

## Headphones and stereo

Check the edition's requirements. Stereo headphones keep the two channels
separate for binaural designs; speakers or a mono accessibility setting change
that presentation. Do not assume every OCF edition needs headphones or that a
different presentation guarantees the same experience.

## Volume

Start low and use a comfortable, clearly audible level. Do not turn up a quiet
passage to chase an effect. Lower the volume or stop if sound feels uncomfortable;
this guide does not prescribe a level from an uncalibrated player slider.

## Focus 10 and Focus 11: design labels

Focus 10 and Focus 11 identify intended stages in OCF session designs, not
verified measurements of your mind or evidence of a guaranteed state. Semantic
stage boundaries and sound transitions are distinct. The chosen edition's plan,
not its filename or a frequency alone, defines its stage sequence. You need not
report any particular sensation to use the guide.

## Repeated listening

If you choose to repeat a recording, allow time to notice comfort and ordinary
day-to-day effects. More repeats are not proof of stronger results. There is no
universal listening schedule supplied by this guide.

## Practice between sessions

Use only the instructions actually documented for your chosen function. Do not
invent a breath hold, gesture, or command sequence from the command's name. Keep
practice optional and compatible with your current surroundings.

## Ongoing functions

The catalog may declare manual activation or ongoing persistence. Those are
metadata descriptions, not a guarantee that an effect starts, continues, or
stops. Do not infer a release command, duration, or trigger when one is missing.
Pausing playback is always available, but is not evidence of a semantic release.

## Manual functions

Manual activation is a catalog classification, not a complete procedure. Read
the edition's reviewed instructions rather than assuming that saying a cue alone
is sufficient. The guide does not prescribe when or how often to activate it.

## Sleep and wakefulness

Check whether the edition is intended for sleep or for a return to wakeful
activity. Do not assume all recordings have the same ending. Allow time to become
alert before resuming activities that require attention.

## Transcripts

Use an edition-matched transcript when available to preview wording or follow
along without audio. A function guide is not a transcript. Source transcripts,
working drafts, and released wording have different review and rights statuses;
missing or private text is not reproduced here.

## Players and playback settings

Use a player that supports the supplied format and seeking. Check whether repeat,
shuffle, crossfade, playback speed, mono, equalization, or normalization is enabled;
these can change timing, endings, or stereo presentation. Disable unwanted repeat
before beginning. A player setting is not OCF approval of that playback path.

## Editions and versions

Keep the function ID, recording edition, locale, and version together. Two files
with similar titles may differ in narration, pacing, voice, or background design.
A generated PDF or packaged audio file does not establish listening approval or
rights clearance. Use the review status attached to the actual edition.

## Accessibility

Choose the text or audio form that works for you. You can read first, pause,
adjust playback, or decline headphones if unsuitable. Markdown remains the source
text; the PDF uses selectable text rather than text images. The PDF is not claimed
to be a tagged or certified accessible document.

## Imagery is not required

There is no need to see pictures in your mind, close your eyes, or imitate
someone else's sensory experience. You may attend to words, meaning, bodily
comfort, or another useful point of attention, without forcing imagery.

## Precautions

OCF is not a substitute for healthcare, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency help.
Read function-specific safety notes and seek appropriate professional advice if
you have concerns about a recording. Stop if distressed, dizzy, or unwell. Do not
use a command as a reason to ignore pain or a safety warning.

## Feedback

When reporting a problem, include function ID, edition/version, locale,
player/settings, and the relevant time or cue. Distinguish what you heard from
what you expected. Share only information you are comfortable disclosing; do not
upload private source transcripts, voice recordings, or health details by default.

## Function guides

The accompanying guides resolve current display and spoken cues, documented
activation steps, secondary-command meanings, and declared lifecycle/mode behavior
from canonical metadata and locale resources. Optional related practices are
shown only where explicitly approved; they are not prerequisites. Guides label
missing data honestly and link included edition files through package navigation.
They do not establish effects, authorize release, or replace review records for a
specific listening edition.

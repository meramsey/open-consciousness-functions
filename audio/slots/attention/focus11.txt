From this moment on, when you want to focus your attention narrowly and closely, all you need do is inhale, hold your breath, and say in your mind, PLUS-FOCUS.
When you do this, your mind and body will direct and hold all your senses, all your thoughts, and all of your thinking into one area of activity, whatever you so desire.
When you do focus your attention by this method, you will remember in great detail all of that which you perceive and experience, and you will feel good, bright, and calmly sure.
All of these will be yours when you inhale deeply and hold your breath and say, PLUS-FOCUS, in your mind, and then exhale through your mouth as if you were blowing out a candle.
The more you perform this exercise of focusing your attention, the easier and more effective it will become.
